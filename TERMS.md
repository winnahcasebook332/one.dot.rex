# Re:X Distribution & Usage Guidelines

Re:X is a closed-source project developed and maintained by 1Dot. Please follow these usage and distribution guidelines:

## 1. Official downloads & sharing

* The official repository is: `https://github.com/Xposed-Modules-Repo/one.dot.rex`
* Please download the APK only through official channels.
* You are welcome to share links to the official project page, Releases, or Telegram group.

## 2. Distribution restrictions

* **Do not redistribute the APK:** Do not re-upload or mirror the Re:X APK to third-party services such as file hosts, forums, download sites, or app stores.
* **Do not distribute modified versions:** Do not modify, repackage, or re-sign the APK, or bundle it into a modified X APK or third-party installer for distribution.

## 3. Platform use

* Please avoid turning Re:X into “big news” on X through coordinated promotion or attempts to attract large-scale attention.
* Casual mentions, sharing your experience, and posting screenshots are fine.

## 4. Disclaimer

* Re:X is an independent third-party project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorized by X Corp.
* Using Re:X may involve compatibility, stability, or account-related risks. Please make your own assessment and assume responsibility for any consequences.
